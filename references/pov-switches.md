# POV-switch anchors

## Core anchors

1. Character name or unmistakable identity.
2. Physical location or spatial position.
3. Sensory input filtered through the viewpoint character.

A transition that clearly hits all three core anchors is adequate: at least 7/10,
usually 8/10 or better.

## Optional anchors

These are supererogatory: useful, but not individually required.

1. Immediate thought or judgment.
2. Distinctive voice or diction.
3. Immediate goal or intention.
4. Emotional state.
5. Relationship orientation toward another character.
6. Time or action continuity.
7. Knowledge boundary—what this viewpoint does and does not know.

Do not invent a defect merely because an optional anchor is absent. Examine whether
the transition is actually confusing in context, especially when a repeated event
from another viewpoint is intentional.
